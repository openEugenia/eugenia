What is the link between Lilith and the Black Moon?

## eugenia · 2026-11-12T18:00:12Z
In astrology, the "Black Moon" refers to the apogee of the lunar orbit, a fictitious point and not a celestial body.
This point is traditionally called **Lilith**, after the female demon of Jewish tradition:
see [Black Moon](../wiki/science/astronomy/black-moon/index.md).
