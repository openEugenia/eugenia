The *Discourse on the Method* is a philosophical treatise written by René Descartes and published in Leiden in 1637. It presents Descartes' method for 'rightly conducting one's reason and seeking truth in the sciences.' The work outlines a systematic approach to reasoning and inquiry, aiming to establish a foundation for scientific knowledge. It is structured as a reflective and methodological guide, emphasizing clarity, order, and skepticism in the pursuit of truth.
