This section explores the origin of the name 'Black Moon Lilith.' It is derived from Lilith, a female demon in Jewish tradition. This connection adds a symbolic and mythological dimension to the astrological concept of the Black Moon.

## Knowledge to extract

### Origin of the name 'Black Moon Lilith'
The name 'Black Moon Lilith' refers to **Lilith**, the female demon of Jewish tradition.

- Question: What is the origin of the name 'Black Moon Lilith'?
- Answer: It refers to Lilith, the female demon of Jewish tradition.
