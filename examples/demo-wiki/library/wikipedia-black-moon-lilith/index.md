Article on the astrologers' Black Moon, the apogee of the lunar orbit. My reading notes:

## A point, not a body

The Black Moon is not a celestial body: it is a point of the Moon's orbit around the Earth, the
**apogee**, where the Moon is farthest from the Earth.

## Mean and true Black Moon

Astrologers compute it in two ways: the **mean Black Moon** follows an averaged orbit of the
Moon, while the **true Black Moon** follows its actual, irregular orbit.

## A slow cycle

Because the orbit of the Moon turns slowly, the Black Moon goes around the zodiac in about
**nine years**.

## The name

Its name refers to **Lilith**, the female demon of Jewish tradition.
