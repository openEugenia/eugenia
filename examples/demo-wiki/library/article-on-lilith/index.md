> Lilith is a female demon of Jewish tradition, who stands for a deity of Mesopotamian
> origin. In the Jewish legends that spread in the Middle Ages, Lilith is presented,
> according to the Alphabet of Ben Sira, as Adam's first wife, before Eve.
